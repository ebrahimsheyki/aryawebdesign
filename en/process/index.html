Process

Website & Software Development: From Idea to Solution

Every project follows real business needs—from understanding the problem and designing the solution to development, testing, deployment, and post-launch support.

The X Web Design Process

  1. Discovery & Analysis

    We examine business goals, users, core requirements, workflows, and project constraints to define the problem clearly.

  2. Solution Design

    We define the product structure, appropriate architecture, priorities, and delivery path around real business needs.

  3. Design & Development

    The interface and technical solution are implemented with a focus on user experience, code quality, security, performance, and maintainability.

  4. Testing & Optimization

    Core features, page behavior, performance, and important issues are reviewed so the final version is ready for release.

  5. Deployment

    The release candidate is deployed to production and its real-world performance is checked after launch.

  6. Support & Growth

    After launch, we can continue with issue resolution, product improvements, and new capabilities as the business evolves.

Delivery Principles

Quality Throughout the Project

A good process is not only about the order of steps; the quality of decisions matters throughout the project.

Transparency

Goals, priorities, and project progress should remain clear and easy for you to follow.

Quality

Security, performance, architecture, and maintainability are considered throughout the project.

Ready to Grow

The solution is built for today while leaving room for future growth.
