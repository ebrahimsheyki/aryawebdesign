FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about services, pricing, delivery, support, and working with X.

What services do you provide?

X provides professional websites, e-commerce, custom software, and enterprise solutions. The right solution is chosen based on real business needs.

How is project pricing determined?

Project pricing depends on the goal, required features, technical complexity, design, integrations, and delivery time. A single fixed price for every project is therefore not practical.

How long does a project take?

Delivery time depends on the project type, feature scope, design needs, integrations, and content readiness. A realistic schedule is defined after the initial analysis.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. After launch, support, issue resolution, and further development can be provided as the project evolves.

Can you develop custom software and APIs?

Yes. For projects with specific requirements, we can analyze, design, and develop custom software, backend services, and APIs around business processes.

What information is needed to start?

Share the project goal, business type, core needs, expected features, and current situation. After an initial review, we will recommend the right path.

Are websites prepared for SEO?

The technical structure can follow SEO principles such as appropriate URLs, canonicalization, internal linking, semantic HTML, and crawlability.

Do you offer Java, Spring, and Spring Boot training?

Yes. Java, Spring, and Spring Boot training is offered as a separate specialized service. See the training page for more information.
